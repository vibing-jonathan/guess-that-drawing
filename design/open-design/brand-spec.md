- Display: `"Nunito Variable", "Nunito", ui-rounded, sans-serif`.
- Body/UI: `"DM Sans Variable", "DM Sans", system-ui, sans-serif`.
- Numeric: DM Sans with tabular figures; no separate monospace face.
- The atmosphere is a well-used sketchbook on a lively tabletop: warm, crafted, confident, and social.
- Pure white is reserved for the drawing canvas so the creative surface always reads first.
- Cobalt owns primary action and turn progress; coral is an energetic accent, not a second primary.
- Hand-drawn character comes from sturdy outlines and small offset shadows, never random rotation or distressed readability.
- Gameplay surfaces stay quiet: no gradients, glass, fake 3D, decorative clutter, or color-only meaning.
